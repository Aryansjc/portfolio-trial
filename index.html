THE COLLECTED WORKS OF A. RIVERA§ 1 · INTRODUCTION

§ 1Introduction

The author has spent the better part of a decade converting ambiguous briefs into working software — a process the literature calls “design” and the author's clients call “Tuesday”. The work collected here spans analytics platforms, identity systems, and small tools made for large problems.1

Three claims organize this portfolio: craft compounds; motion is argument; and deadlines are a load-bearing structure, to be respected as such.
